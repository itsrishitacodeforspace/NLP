1. Definitions

1.1 "Personal Data" has the meaning given in the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. "Data Protection Laws" means the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules and orders made under it.

2. Processing Obligations

2.1 The Processor shall process Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Controller and only to the extent required to perform its obligations under the master services agreement.

2.2 The Processor shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect Personal Data, including encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access controls and audit logging.

3. Data Breach Notification

3.1 The Processor shall notify the Controller of any personal data breach or security incident without undue delay and in any event within twenty-four (24) hours of becoming aware of it. The notification shall describe the nature of the breach, the categories of Personal Data affected, and the mitigation measures taken or proposed.

3.2 Each party shall cooperate with the other in meeting its notification duties to the Data Protection Board of India under the Data Protection Laws.

4. Cross-Border Transfer

4.1 The Processor shall not transfer Personal Data to any country outside India except with the prior written consent of the Controller, and only where the transfer is required for the performance of the master services agreement.

5. Term and Survival

5.1 This Agreement takes effect on the Effective Date and continues for so long as the Processor processes Personal Data on the Controller's behalf. Sections 2, 3 and 4 shall survive termination or expiry of this Agreement.
